Privacy ⋆ ♡ ⋆

Short version: your stuff stays in your browser.

Your bCourses token

Your token is saved only in your own browser (for this tab, or on this device if you tick "remember"). Every sync sends it to a small relay function on this site, which forwards one read-only request to bcourses.berkeley.edu and returns the answer. The relay does not store, log or cache the token or the answer.

The relay exists because bCourses blocks other websites from calling its API from your browser directly. It only allows three read-only endpoints: your profile, your active courses, and your planner (to-do) items. It cannot submit, post, message or change anything.

Everything else

Your checkmarks, your own quests, your pet's name and a copy of your synced assignments are saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere else. There are no accounts, analytics or ads.

Taking it back

Click "disconnect" to clear the token and cached assignments from this browser. To revoke the token everywhere, delete it in bCourses under Account → Settings → Approved Integrations. Tokens with an expiration date stop working on their own.

The fine print

Crunchling is a student project. It is not affiliated with UC Berkeley or Instructure. A bCourses token can read everything your account can, so only paste it into tools you trust, and set an expiration date.
