hatch your crunchling ✧

Connect your bCourses so your assignments become quests. Exams become boss fights.

  1. In bCourses, open Account → Settings.
  2. Under Approved Integrations, click + New Access Token. Purpose: crunchling. Set an expiration date (end of semester is good).
  3. Copy the token and paste it here.

Where your token goes. It stays in this browser. When the page syncs, it passes through a small read-only relay (needed because bCourses blocks other websites from calling it directly) and is never stored or logged. Only three read-only bCourses endpoints are allowed: your profile, your courses, and your to-do planner. You can delete the token anytime in bCourses settings. Privacy details
